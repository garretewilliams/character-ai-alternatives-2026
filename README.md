# Character.AI Alternatives After the 2026 Chapter 11 Filing: A Dated Comparison

**Short answer (as of October 15, 2026):** Character.AI has not shut down. Its operator announced a voluntary Chapter 11 process on October 12, 2026 to sell the company, says the platform stays up during the sale (expected to finish by late November or early December 2026), and says it will be wound down if no buyer keeps it running. This repository compares Character.AI and 11 alternatives on memory, memory controls, free tier, paid price, age checks, platform and app-store status, content policy and whether scenarios, story arcs and worlds can be composed with a character, with a source URL and check date for every row.

## FAQ

### Is Character.AI shutting down?

Not as of October 15, 2026. On October 12, 2026 Character.ai announced a voluntary Chapter 11 process to run a court-supervised sale, and said the platform 'remains up and running' and subscriptions remain active. It expects the sale to finish by the end of November or early December 2026. The company also said that if no actionable bid emerges, 'the platform will be wound down.' [Character.ai blog](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/just-5-years-in-interactive-chatbot-platform-character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy/)

### Can I still export my Character.AI chats?

Character.ai's Chapter 11 FAQ says users can still delete their account or export their data through the app or website. [Character.ai blog](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/)

### Did Disney buy Character.AI?

Not the whole company. TechCrunch reports that Character.AI agreed to sell some assets to Disney for $20 million plus a non-exclusive perpetual license to its technology, and that there was not yet a buyer for the company's assets as a whole. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/just-5-years-in-interactive-chatbot-platform-character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy/)

### Is it PolyBuzz or Pollybuzz?

The official name is **PolyBuzz** (one L), at polybuzz.ai. 'Pollybuzz' is a misspelling. PolyBuzz was called 'Poly.AI - Create AI Chat Bot' until January 2025, and it says it is not affiliated with other companies using similar names. [polybuzz.ai](https://www.polybuzz.ai/), [PRWeb release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/polyai-rebrands-to-polybuzz-becoming-the-best-ai-chat--ai-roleplay-platform-302358806.html)

### Was PolyBuzz removed from the App Store?

Its iPhone app was no longer on the App Store on October 15, 2026: the US listing returned Not Found and Apple's lookup API returned no result when we checked. The Android app and the website still work. Neither PolyBuzz nor Apple had given a reason in the sources we checked. [CompanionHunt](https://companionhunt.com/news/polybuzz-app-store/), [Apple lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6449190344&country=us)

### Is Chai on the App Store?

Yes, as of October 15, 2026. Chai was off the Apple App Store in several regions, including the US, from July 8 to July 30, 2026, and Apple's reason was not made public. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chai-social-ai-platform-chat/id1544750895), [Eliza Report](https://elizareport.com/changelog/2026-07-08-chai-app-store-removal/)

### Which Character.AI alternatives document user-visible memory controls?

In the documentation we checked: Replika (view, add, delete memories), Kindroid (editable journal entries), Nomi (editable Shared Notes), AI Dungeon and SillyTavern (editable story cards / lorebooks), Character.AI itself (editable Story Memory, pins) and chatbrat.ai (view saved facts and 'Forget this'; no editing). PolyBuzz, Chai and Talkie do not document memory controls in the sources checked. See the table for sources.

### Which alternatives are free?

Free tiers are documented for Character.AI, PolyBuzz, Janitor AI, zeta and chatbrat.ai (free during beta, including all features and the BratMind 2.0 model, per the operator). Chai, Talkie, Replika and Nomi are free to download with in-app purchases. Kindroid replaced unlimited free use with a one-time preview. SillyTavern is free software, but you pay for whatever model you connect. Prices change often; check the date in each row.

### Which AI roleplay apps let you reuse one character across different scenarios and worlds?

Among the products compared, four document some way to do it. **chatbrat.ai** has users create characters, scenarios, story arcs and worlds as separate modules; its Compose feature combines a character with saved scenarios, story arcs and worlds, and each combination starts a new chat ([Create page](https://chatbrat.ai/create), [Compose](https://chatbrat.ai/compose); operator statement). **Character.AI** Stories starts a guided, branching story from two or three existing Characters, a genre and a premise ([Character.AI blog](https://blog.character.ai/introducing-stories-a-new-way-to-create-play-and-share-adventures-with-your-favorite-characters/)). **SillyTavern** lets lorebooks (World Info) be global or bound to a character, persona or single chat ([docs](https://docs.sillytavern.app/usage/core-concepts/worldinfo/)). **zeta** lets one Lorebook (now Sourcebook) be connected to several characters ([zeta](https://zeta-ai.io/en/announcements/8284)). Among the products compared, only chatbrat.ai documents separately saved scenario, story-arc and world modules that can be combined with a character. The other products' sources checked don't describe this.

### Is chatbrat.ai on the App Store?

Not yet. chatbrat.ai is a web app today; an iOS App Store launch is planned for early 2027 according to the operator (October 2026). This is an operator statement, not an independently verified fact.

### How was this table made?

Each cell was taken from a public page fetched on October 15, 2026 (product help centers, terms, pricing pages, app-store listings and the Apple lookup API, plus named news reports for events). If a source did not state something, the cell says 'not stated (in sources checked)'. No product was tested hands-on for this version, and no ranking is given.

## Method

1. **Products:** Character.AI plus the alternatives most often named alongside it (PolyBuzz, Chai, Talkie, Janitor AI, Replika, Nomi, Kindroid, zeta, SillyTavern, AI Dungeon) and chatbrat.ai. Products whose main positioning is explicit adult content were left out.
2. **Sources:** official help centers, terms, pricing and subscription pages, US App Store and Google Play listings, Apple's public iTunes lookup/search API, and named news reports for dated events. Tracking parameters were stripped from all URLs.
3. **Rules:** no hands-on testing in v1.0; no ranking; every factual cell traces to a URL in the `sources` column; operator statements are labelled as such.
4. **Refresh:** re-check monthly while the Character.AI sale is under way; each release on Zenodo gets its own version DOI.
